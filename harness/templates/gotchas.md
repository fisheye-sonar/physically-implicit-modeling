data-generation quirks, and "if you see X, check Y first" diagnostics.

**This is not the findings record** (that is what is *true* about the research) and **not the
harness** (that is how to work anywhere). This is what will silently waste a day.

Newest first. Every entry dated. Delete an entry only when the trap no longer exists — and
say so in the entry rather than removing the history.
